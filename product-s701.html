三片式球閥 · Three-Piece Ball Valve

S701

模組化三片式，平頭閥桿驅動。

S701 系列為三片式球閥，採平頭閥桿驅動與防噴出閥桿設計。三片式結構提供模組化的閥門配置，適用於工業關斷用途；平頭閥桿則提供直接的機械驅動介面。

The Series S701 is identified as a three-piece ball valve with a flat-head stem drive and a blowout-proof stem design. Its three-piece construction provides a modular valve arrangement for industrial shut-off service, while the flat-head stem creates a direct mechanical drive interface.

Product Features
