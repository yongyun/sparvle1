一片式球閥 · One-Piece Ball Valve

E301-30

一片式外牙接頭，精巧經濟。

E301 系列為一片式球閥，專為工業與化學應用設計。採縮徑流道，在一般壓力條件下提供穩定可靠的表現。精巧的免維護結構，為液體與氣體系統的手動開關控制提供經濟的解決方案，適用於隔離點、排放管線與測試應用，並可依使用需求選擇多種球墊材質。

The Series E301 is a one-piece valve engineered for industrial and chemical applications. It features a reduced-bore flow path and dependable performance under standard pressure conditions. Its compact, non-maintainable construction provides an economical solution for manual on/off control in liquid and gas systems, including isolation points, drainage lines, and testing applications. Multiple seat materials can be selected to suit different service requirements.
