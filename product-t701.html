逆止閥 · Check Valve

T701

彈簧式逆止閥，守護系統完整。

T701 系列為三片式彈簧逆止閥，可維持液體單向流動並有效防止逆流。耐用的金屬閥座搭配彈簧閥瓣，可任意方向安裝以配合各種系統配置；低開啟壓力讓運轉更有效率，可互換的端蓋則可依不同連接需求配置。適用於需要確保流向以保護系統完整性、避免設備損壞的應用。

The Series T701 is a three-piece, spring-loaded check valve engineered to maintain one-way liquid flow and effectively prevent backflow. Featuring a durable metal seat and spring-loaded disc, it can be installed in any orientation to accommodate a variety of system configurations. Its low opening pressure supports efficient operation, while interchangeable end caps allow the valve to be configured for different connection requirements. The Series T701 is suitable for applications where reliable flow direction is essential to protecting system integrity and preventing equipment damage.
